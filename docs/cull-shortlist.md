## What's in the take

One athlete, one evening shoot, low golden sun. Three wardrobe/setup states:

1. **Black compression shirt, no helmet, flat light, park** — `DSC_8877`–`8900`.
   Casual top, midday-flat light, cluttered tree line with cars and houses.
   Not Direction A. Cut as a group; one kept as a face-visible alternate.
2. **Full maroon Rattlers game jersey (#13), helmet on, warm low sun** —
   `DSC_8902`–`8959`. The strongest material. Standing hero poses, attitude
   (finger-to-visor), power (flex, seated wide stance), back-of-jersey, one
   helmet-raised-to-sky. This is where the hero set comes from.
3. **Action — running with the ball, low sun, open field** — `DSC_8982`–`9113`.
   Many near-duplicates. Best have real stride separation, the ball reading
   clearly, a warm rim on the visor, and a background that isolates cleanly.
   The far, small-in-frame runs (`9046`, `9053`, `9057`, `9059`, `9060`,
   `9062`, `9088`–`9090`) are cut.

## Honest flags before you lock this

- **The face is hidden in almost every strong frame.** The helmet is on with a
  dark visor in every group-2 and group-3 pick. The only clean face-visible
  frames are `DSC_8906` (game face, helmet held low — the proposed signature),
  `DSC_8907` (same, smiling), and `DSC_8885` (no helmet). For a site whose buyer
  is a parent, that's thin. Two options: (a) lean into it — the visor-down
  anonymity actually suits the poster grammar in `brand.md`, and the wordmark
  plus the number carry identity; or (b) plan a short reshoot for three or four
  clean helmet-off portraits in the game jersey. Recommend (a) for v1, (b) as an
  early post-launch add with the second shoot already flagged in the plan.
- **Every pick is the same uniform, same light, same session.** Good for a
  cohesive graded set; it also means the gallery has one note. The compositing
  in Phase 2 (varied stadium backgrounds, different crops and scales) is what
  creates range, so vary those deliberately.
- **Team marks are visible** — "RATTLERS", "GYA", the number. Fine for this
  athlete's own portfolio use; keep it in mind if a frame ever gets reused as
  generic marketing.
- **Light stands and an apple box are in some frames** (`9081`–`9083`, `8943`,
  `8944`). All removable in the composite; noted so they're not a surprise.

## Cutout pass + re-score (session 4)

Ran background removal on all 14 picks (Adobe / Photoshop `select-subject`
cutout) plus two extras for a swap decision. Transparent PNGs are in
`design/cull/nobg/` as `DSC_XXXX_nobg.png`. Review sheets in `design/cull/`:
`CUTOUTS.jpg` (all 14 on a checkerboard), `CUTZOOM.jpg` (edge close-ups),
`SWAPCHECK.jpg` (the back/flex frames).

These are evaluation cutouts from the 1200px proxies — good enough to judge
edge behaviour and to drop into a wireframe. The real cutouts get redone in
Photoshop from full-res masters during compositing.

### What the isolation revealed

- **Edge quality is strong.** The auto-cutout handled hair (`8906`), facemask
  bar gaps (`8931`, `8902`), gloves, and the ball cleanly. The
  standing/attitude frames are effectively portfolio-grade with no rework;
  the fast-moving foot or trailing arm in the run/catch frames is slightly
  soft, which is expected and reads fine at speed.
- **Two flagged compositing costs disappeared.** `DSC_8944`'s apple box and
  `DSC_9039`'s chain-link fence were both excluded by the subject detection —
  `9039` was the single riskiest cut in the set and it's now clean.
- **The whole set is front-facing.** Re-checking the originals, `8902`,
  `8925`, `8926`, and `9065` are all shot from the **front** (facemask and
  visor visible) — the earlier "back of jersey" labels were wrong. There is
  no back-of-number shot in the 14. If you want one, it exists in the wider
  take — `DSC_8914` and `DSC_8964` show the rear "GYA 13" nameplate — pull one
  from there.
- **`DSC_9065`'s raised hands are clipped by the original frame.** Fine for a
  full-bleed graphic where the top crops anyway; as a free-standing cutout the
  fingertips look cut. Keep it as the closer, compose around it.

### One swap

**`DSC_8926` → `DSC_8902`.** `8926` is the third near-identical front
double-bicep flex and the weakest lit of them. `8902` (same setup, one white
glove splayed open against the dark visor, forearms crossed over the "13") is
a genuinely different, more graphic image and cuts just as cleanly. If you'd
rather keep the straight flex, `DSC_8925` is the better-framed version of it.
