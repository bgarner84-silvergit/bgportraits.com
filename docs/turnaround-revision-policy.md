## Turnaround

The quick cutout is fast per client, but batching and your actual weekly hours (not
unlimited) set the real pace for the full composite. Proposed:

| Deliverable | Turnaround |
|---|---|
| Digital cutout + AI-background instructions | 2 business days from the session |
| Future Star — 1 hero composite | 10 business days (~2 weeks) |
| Future Legend — 3 hero composites | 15 business days (~3 weeks) |
| Champion's Legacy — 5 hero composites | 20 business days (~4 weeks) for the digital set |
| Champion's Legacy — framed big print | +5–10 business days after composite approval, to account for print/framing production and shipping (separate from your labor, but still real client-facing wait time) |

These assume you're working one job at a time. If you're ever running two Champion's
Legacy orders concurrently, the honest move is to say so up front rather than quietly slip
both — worth deciding now whether the site should show a "current turnaround" estimate
that moves with your actual queue, or a fixed number that's padded to survive a normal
backlog.

## Revisions

- **One included round of revisions per hero composite** — color/tone adjustment, crop,
  minor retouch notes on the delivered version.
- **Request window: 7 days from delivery.** After that, the composite is considered final
  and accepted. This isn't about being rigid — it's what makes "1 round" a real boundary
  instead of an open-ended commitment.
- **A different pose, a different background concept, or a materially different look is a
  new composite, not a revision** — billed at the variant rate ($230 hard cost basis from
  the cost sheet), same as any additional composite. This keeps the revision policy from
  quietly becoming a second free composite.
- **Additional revision rounds beyond the included one:** billed at your hourly rate for
  time spent, $100/hr, billed in 30-minute increments. No separate "revision fee" schedule
  to maintain — it's just your rate, applied.

## Print & framing errors

- **Your production error** (wrong size, printing defect, framing damage in your control):
  free reprint, no discussion needed.
- **Client-requested change after production has started** (size change, swap which
  composite gets printed, reprint after approval): client covers the reprint at your cost
  plus shipping. Not a penalty — just not something you should absorb.
