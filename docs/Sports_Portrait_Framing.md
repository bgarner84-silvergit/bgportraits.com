# Sports Portrait Framing & Fulfillment SOP

**Document Purpose:** Internal standard operating procedure and pricing reference for 16x20" sports portrait composites. This guide establishes the baseline museum-grade archival standards required for premium client deliverables.

## Baseline Specifications
For high-impact senior football composites and dynamic sports graphics printed on luster or heavy matte stock, the following materials represent the default studio standard for a 16x20" print (resulting in a 20x24" final frame).

*   **Default Frame Style:** Matte Black Gallery Profile
*   **Default Glazing:** Museum Glass®
*   **Default Matting:** 100% Cotton Rag Matboard
*   **Default Mounting:** Fully reversible (Archival photo corners or Japanese hinging paper with wheat starch paste. *Never use permanent adhesive sprays.*)

## Premium Default Build & Cost Breakdown

| Component | Selection | Estimated Cost | Why It's the Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frame Moulding** | Matte Black Gallery Profile | $80 – $130 | A deep black frame disappears visually, forcing all attention onto the saturated colors, high-contrast edits, and dynamic lighting of the athlete. |
| **UV Glazing** | Museum Glass® | $90 – $150 | 99% UV protection. The anti-reflective coating allows full detail, texture, and color clarity to shine through without distracting glare. |
| **Matboard** | 100% Cotton Rag | $60 – $100+ | Naturally acid-free and exceptionally durable. Ensures the heirloom quality of the print with zero risk of chemical breakdown or edge burning over time. |
| **Backing** | Acid-Free Foamcore | $10 – $20 | The required baseline backing to prevent acid migration from the rear of the frame. |
| **Total Est. Material** | **Standard Premium Build** | **$240 – $400** | *Excludes local shop labor/assembly charges (typically $50+).* |

## Vendor Tier Reference

When outsourcing fulfillment, quotes should be sourced based on the required turnaround time and material availability. 

### 1. Independent Custom Frame Shops (e.g., Art Impressions, uberphat)
*   **Vibe:** Premium craftsmanship, strict conservation methods, personalized design.
*   **Expected Cost (Full Archival Build):** $250 – $400+
*   **Best For:** Your highest-tier client deliverables and complex mounting needs (ensuring strict adherence to reversible mounting).

### 2. Big-Box Retailers (e.g., Michaels Custom Framing)
*   **Vibe:** Volume-driven, budget-friendly when sales are active.
*   **Expected Cost (Full Archival Build):** $150 – $250 (highly dependent on 70% off promotional windows).
*   **Best For:** Standardizing margins on mid-tier packages, provided you explicitly enforce the archival mounting requirements with the framing desk.
