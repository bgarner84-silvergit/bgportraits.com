# Likeness release — minor (plain template)

**This is a plain-language starting template, not legal advice.** Because the
subject is a minor and the images are intended for paid advertising, have a
licensed attorney in your state review this before you rely on it. Fill the
bracketed fields, delete this notice, and print it on business letterhead.

---

## Photograph and likeness release

**Photographer / business:** [Brandon Garner Photography — registered business
name], [address], [phone], [email] ("Photographer").

**Minor:** [full name of minor] ("the Minor"), date of birth [MM/DD/YYYY].

**Parent or legal guardian:** [full name] ("Guardian"), relationship to the
Minor: [parent / legal guardian].

### 1. Grant of rights

The Guardian, on behalf of the Minor and themselves, grants the Photographer the
irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free right to use, reproduce, edit,
adapt, publish, and display the photographs taken at the session on or about
[shoot date] ("the Images"), together with the Minor's first name or a first
name and last initial, for:

- the Photographer's portfolio, website, and social media accounts;
- print, digital, and social advertising and other marketing of the
  Photographer's services;
- submission to photography competitions, publications, and features.

The grant includes composited and retouched versions of the Images. The
Photographer is under no obligation to use the Images.

### 2. What the Guardian receives

The photography session and the delivered portrait(s) are the full
consideration for this release. No other payment is owed to the Guardian or the
Minor now or in the future, regardless of how the Images are used.

### 3. What is not granted

This release does not transfer copyright, which the Photographer retains. It
does not permit use that is defamatory, or use by third parties except as needed
to place the Photographer's own advertising.

### 4. Guardian's authority and acknowledgements

The Guardian represents that they are the parent or legal guardian of the Minor
with authority to sign this release, that they have read it in full, and that
they are signing freely. The Guardian has had the chance to ask questions and to
seek independent legal advice.

### 5. Prior approval on file

The Guardian's text-message approval dated [date of SMS], attached as Exhibit A,
is incorporated as evidence of consent and does not replace this signed release.

### 6. General

This release is governed by the laws of the State of [Texas]. If any part is
found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. This document is the entire
agreement between the parties about the Images.
