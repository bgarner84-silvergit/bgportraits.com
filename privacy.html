Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Brandon Garner Photography ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

When you join our early access list, contact us, or use the chat feature on this site, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide it
  • Any messages you send us through our contact or chat forms

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and booking requests
  • Notify you when our site, booking, or services go live
  • Send you updates about our photography services, if you have opted in
  • Send text messages (SMS) related to your inquiry or booking, if you have provided your phone number and consented to receive them

SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your phone number and opt in to receive text messages, we may send you messages related to scheduling, booking confirmations, and updates about our services. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by contacting us directly using the information below. We do not sell or share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate this website and manage client communications (for example, our customer relationship management and scheduling platform), solely for the purpose of providing our services to you.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and out of emails by using the unsubscribe link included in our messages. You may also contact us directly to request that we delete your information.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
